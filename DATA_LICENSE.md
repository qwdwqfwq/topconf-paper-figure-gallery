It does not license the paper figures in `images/`, the papers, or publisher-owned material; those remain subject to the rights and licenses of their original authors and publishers. 本文件适用于画廊元数据及其衍生索引，包括 `data/figures.json`、`assets/figures.js`、会议/年份标签、视觉模式标签、录取等级标签、来源链接，以及这些文件所体现的精选与编排。本文件不授予 `images/` 中论文图片、论文正文或出版方材料的许可；这些内容仍受原论文作者和出版方的权利与许可约束，详见 [IMAGES_POLICY.md](IMAGES_POLICY.md)。

## Required attribution / 必须引用

If you use this project's metadata, curated index, labels, selection, or derived metadata in research, teaching, a benchmark, a model-training corpus, or a paper, you must:

1. cite this project in the paper or accompanying technical documentation;
2. acknowledge the project and its curator in the paper's acknowledgements section; and
3. include the repository URL and identify the original paper/proceedings sources used by the relevant records.

如果你在科研、教学、基准测试、模型训练语料或论文中使用本项目的元数据、精选索引、标签、筛选结果或衍生元数据，必须：

1. 在论文或配套技术文档中引用本项目；
2. 在论文致谢中注明使用了本项目及其维护者整理的元数据；
3. 注明仓库链接，并说明相关记录所对应的原始论文/会议出版来源。

Repository / 仓库：<https://github.com/qwdwqfwq/topconf-paper-figure-gallery>
