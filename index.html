About this gallery · 关于本画廊

Every image is the Figure 1 / Teaser rendered from the officially published PDF (~180–216 DPI), sourced from OpenReview (ICLR), PMLR (ICML), NeurIPS Proceedings, CVF Open Access (CVPR), ACL Anthology, AAAI Press and arXiv. Each card links to the paper and lists title and authors; copyright belongs to the authors and publishers.

所有图片均提取自正式发表论文的 Figure 1 / Teaser（PDF 渲染，约 180–216 DPI）， 每张卡片均标注论文标题、全部作者与原文链接，版权归原作者及出版方所有。 ICLR / ICML(PMLR) / NeurIPS / CVPR / ACL 正式出版论文多以 CC BY 4.0 许可发布； 本画廊仅用于学习、研究与配图灵感参考，不用于商业目的。若您是版权方并认为收录不当， 请联系 939123836@qq.com / open a GitHub issue，我们会在核实后 72 小时内删除。
